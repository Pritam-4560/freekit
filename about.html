About FitFile

FitFile is a collection of free, simple tools for the small jobs that come up every day: shrinking a photo to fit a form, joining PDFs, working out a loan payment, converting units and more.

The big difference: everything runs inside your browser. Your photos, PDFs and numbers are processed on your own device and are never uploaded to a server. There is no signup and no watermark.

Calculators give estimates to help you plan. They are not tax, legal or financial advice. Rules and rates change, so check official sources before you rely on a result. Rates and rules were last verified in November 2026.
