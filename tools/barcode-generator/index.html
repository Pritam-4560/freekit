Questions

Why does my barcode fail?

Each type has rules: EAN-13 needs 12 or 13 digits, EAN-8 needs 7 or 8, UPC-A needs 11 or 12, and Code 39 allows only capital letters, digits and a few symbols. Code 128 accepts any text.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. FitFile runs entirely in your browser, so your files and numbers never leave your device.
