    Questions

    Is my photo uploaded?

    No. The image is processed in your browser and never leaves your device.

    Does it work with HEIC photos from iPhone?

    Yes, HEIC and HEIF files are converted in your browser first. If one fails, set iPhone Camera to Most Compatible or share the photo as JPG.

    What quality should I pick?

    70 to 80 is usually a good balance: much smaller with little visible change.

    Are my files uploaded anywhere?

    No. FitFile runs entirely in your browser, so your files and numbers never leave your device.
