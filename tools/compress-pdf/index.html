Compress PDF

Reduce a PDF to a target size like 100 KB, 500 KB or 2 MB for forms and email. Free, no watermark, nothing uploaded.

    How to use

    1. Choose your PDF.
    2. Pick a limit like "Under 500 KB" or type your own.
    3. Tap Compress and download.

    About this tool

    This tool redraws each page as an image at lower quality until the file fits your limit, so text becomes part of the picture. That gives the smallest files, but text can no longer be selected. Use it for forms and uploads where size matters most.

    Questions

    Is my PDF uploaded?

    No. PDFs are processed in your browser and never leave your device.

    Can it open password-protected PDFs?

    No. Remove the password in the app that made the file, then try again.

    Why can I not select text after compressing?

    To reach small sizes, pages are saved as images. If you need selectable text, use a higher size limit or keep the original.

    Are my files uploaded anywhere?

    No. FitFile runs entirely in your browser, so your files and numbers never leave your device.
