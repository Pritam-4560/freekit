Questions

1000 or 1024?

Operating systems like Windows count 1 KB as 1024 bytes. Storage makers and macOS count 1 KB as 1000 bytes. That is why a "1 TB" drive shows less on Windows.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. FitFile runs entirely in your browser, so your files and numbers never leave your device.
