Questions

What is the gratuity formula?

Gratuity = last drawn monthly basic + DA × 15 ÷ 26 × completed years of service. Six months or more in the last year is rounded up to a full year.

Who gets gratuity?

Usually employees with 5 years of continuous service in an organisation covered by the Payment of Gratuity Act. Rules for fixed-term staff and exceptions differ, so check your employer policy.
