Questions

What are the GST rates now?

Most goods and services are at 5% or 18%, with 40% on a few sin and luxury items, 3% on gold and 0.25% on rough diamonds, effective 22 September 2025. Check the GST portal for your item.

What is CGST and SGST?

For sales within one state, GST splits equally into CGST (Centre) and SGST (State). For sales between states it is charged as IGST.
