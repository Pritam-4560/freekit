Questions

Does this confirm the business exists?

No. It checks the format and checksum only, which catches typing mistakes and many fake numbers. To confirm a business is registered, use the official GST portal search.

How is the checksum worked out?

The 15th character is calculated from the first 14 using a base-36 weighted formula. If the characters do not produce it, the number has a typo.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. FitFile runs entirely in your browser, so your files and numbers never leave your device.
