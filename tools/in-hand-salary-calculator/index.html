Questions

Which regime is better?

The calculator shows both. The new regime wins for most people without large deductions. The old regime can win if you claim high HRA, home loan interest and 80C together.

What is assumed?

Standard deduction of ₹75,000 (new) and ₹50,000 (old), 4% cess, employee PF at 12% of basic, rebate and marginal relief. Surcharge above ₹50 lakh is not modelled. Rules last verified in November 2026. Check the Income Tax Department site before filing.
