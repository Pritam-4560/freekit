Questions

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Everything runs in your browser. Your data never leaves your device.

How strong is it?

The strength meter shows entropy in bits. 60 bits is decent, 80 or more is very strong. Length matters more than symbols.

Should I reuse it?

No. Use a different password for every account, and a password manager to remember them.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. FitFile runs entirely in your browser, so your files and numbers never leave your device.
