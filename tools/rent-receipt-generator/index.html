Questions

Do I need a landlord PAN?

If total rent in a year is over ₹1 lakh, your employer may ask for the landlord PAN to allow HRA exemption.

Is a revenue stamp needed?

Customarily a ₹1 revenue stamp is stuck on cash receipts above ₹5,000. Many employers accept signed receipts for bank payments without one. Ask your employer.

Why English letters only?

The PDF uses a standard font that supports English letters and numbers. Write names in English.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. FitFile runs entirely in your browser, so your files and numbers never leave your device.
