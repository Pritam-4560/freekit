Resize Image to KB

Reduce a JPG or PNG to an exact size limit like 20 KB, 50 KB or 200 KB for forms and uploads. Free, private, nothing uploaded.

    How to use

    1. Choose your photo.
    2. Pick a limit like "Under 100 KB", or type your own. Add a minimum if the form needs one.
    3. Optionally set a width and height in pixels.
    4. Tap Create, then download.

    About this tool

    Many application forms and portals reject photos that are too large or too small. This tool shrinks the file to fit inside your limit while keeping it as sharp as possible, by lowering quality first and pixels only when needed. Set both a minimum and a maximum if the form asks for a range such as 20 to 50 KB.

    Questions

    Is my photo uploaded?

    No. The image is processed in your browser and never leaves your device.

    Does it work with HEIC photos from iPhone?

    Yes, HEIC and HEIF files are converted in your browser first. If one fails, set iPhone Camera to Most Compatible or share the photo as JPG.

    Why is my result smaller than my minimum?

    Simple images, like a plain signature, compress to very small files. Enter a larger width and height so there are more pixels to keep.

    Are my files uploaded anywhere?

    No. FitFile runs entirely in your browser, so your files and numbers never leave your device.
